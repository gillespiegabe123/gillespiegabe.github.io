Selected work

Econometrics / Policy Analysis

A Panel Analysis of Income Inequality and Homelessness

Analyzed the relationship between income inequality and homelessness across U.S. states from 2010-2023 using HUD, ACS, Census, and unemployment data. Built a state-level panel dataset and used fixed-effects regression to evaluate how inequality, unemployment, and housing stock relate to homelessness rates.

  • Cleaned and merged public datasets from HUD, ACS, Census, and unemployment sources
  • Built a balanced state-year panel dataset covering 50 states from 2010-2023
  • Used fixed-effects regression to analyze relationships between inequality, unemployment, housing stock, and homelessness
Public Finance / Research Design

Evaluating the Economic Impacts of Universal Basic Income

Reviewed economic and policy literature on Universal Basic Income, focusing on fiscal sustainability, labor market effects, inequality, welfare outcomes, and public finance trade-offs. Proposed a difference-in-differences research design using the Alaska Permanent Fund Dividend to study business formation.

  • Synthesized research from UBI experiments and cash-transfer programs in Alaska, Finland, Ontario, Kenya, and the U.S.
  • Evaluated labor market, welfare, inequality, and fiscal sustainability implications
  • Proposed an empirical research design using difference-in-differences
