A Panel Analysis of Income Inequality and Homelessness

Analyzed the relationship between income inequality and homelessness across U.S. states from 2010-2023 using HUD, ACS, Census, and unemployment data. Built a state-level panel dataset and used fixed-effects regression to evaluate how inequality, unemployment, and housing stock relate to homelessness rates.

  • Used fixed-effects regression to analyze relationships between inequality, unemployment, housing stock, and homelessness

Health Economics / Policy Analysis

Health Economics Policy Recommendation: Cancer Treatment Coverage

Analyzed a discrete choice experiment on cancer treatment preferences using an individual fixed-effects regression model. Estimated how treatment regimen, visit frequency, overall survival, and out-of-pocket cost influenced patient choice, then used the results to discuss willingness to pay, actuarially fair insurance premiums, moral hazard, adverse selection, and insurance design.

  • Ran an individual fixed-effects regression to analyze treatment choice preferences
  • Interpreted regimen, survival, visit frequency, and out-of-pocket cost coefficients
  • Used willingness-to-pay logic to evaluate treatment value and insurance affordability
  • Developed a policy recommendation for structuring cancer treatment coverage

Skills: Health economics, policy analysis, R, regression, fixed effects, discrete choice experiment, willingness to pay, insurance economics
